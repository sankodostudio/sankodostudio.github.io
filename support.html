Sankodo Studio

Support

Write the chords, watch them turn to light. Sankodo Studio is a desktop sketchbook for chord progressions, melodies and grooves that renders them into audio-reactive video.

Contact

Email dsdevsupport@gmail.com. Please include your app version (click the version number at the top of the app), your computer (Mac or Windows and its version), and what happened. A saved project (.sankodo) or the file you imported helps a lot.

System requirements

  • macOS 12 Monterey or later, Apple Silicon or Intel
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Common questions

Which files can I import? MIDI (.mid), MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) and Guitar Pro (.gp, .gp3–.gp7, .gpx), including files downloaded from Songsterr.

What can I export? MP4 video (H.264 and AAC, using your computer's own encoder), WAV and MP3 audio, and MIDI.

An imported song shows rests or the wrong chords. In the import window, check the role given to each part (melody, chords, bass, drums) and try another chord source or rhythm. Choosing the parts that play the chords usually fixes it.

Where are my projects? Use Save to keep a project as a .sankodo file wherever you like; Open brings it back.

Does it need the internet? Only to check your license key: once when you enter it, then every few days when you're online. Everything else runs on your computer, and it keeps working offline for 45 days at a time.

Where's my license key? In your Gumroad receipt email, and in your Gumroad Library (gumroad.com/library). Paste it into the app when it asks, or click the version number at the top of the app → Enter license key.

New computer? One license works on up to 3 computers. To move it to another one beyond that, email us and we'll reset it.

What does the free trial include? Everything except exporting, with the Sankodo Studio watermark shown. A license key unlocks exporting and removes the watermark.
