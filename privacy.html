Sankodo Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

Sankodo Studio does not collect, store or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your music and files never leave your computer. The only time the app goes online is to check a license key.

What stays on your computer

Your compositions, settings and the files you import (MIDI, MusicXML, Guitar Pro) are processed only on your device. Projects and exports (video, audio, MIDI) are saved only where you choose. Sankodo Studio never uploads them anywhere.

Licence keys

When you enter a license key, and every few days afterwards when you are online, the app checks the key with Gumroad (gumroad.com), where Sankodo Studio is sold. It sends only the key. Gumroad counts how many computers have activated it, so one license can be limited to a few computers. Nothing else is sent. Gumroad handles this under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Sankodo Studio is sold through Gumroad, which takes payment, handles sales tax and sends your receipt, download and license key under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can provide your license and support; we never receive your card details and never sell or share your information.

Children

Sankodo Studio does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
